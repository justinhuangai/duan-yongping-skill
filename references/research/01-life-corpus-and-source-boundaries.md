# Life, Corpus, and Source Boundaries

## What the local archive establishes

The retained corpus is small and mediated. It contains one mirrored Q&A discussion, one archive listing, one podcast episode description, one secondary news article, and an excerpt from a third-party PDF compilation. It is not a complete record of Duan's public statements or a verified biography.

The [Sina article](../sources/articles/sina-no-list.md) describes an operator-to-investor trajectory involving Xiaobawang, BBK, and concentrated investments. Its founder labels, successor relationships, returns, and current-holdings language are secondary claims. Do not turn them into a precise corporate ownership map or present-day portfolio without separate verification.

The [episode description](../sources/articles/apple-podcast-episode-page.md) lists chapters about career decisions, culture, delegation, business understanding, and opportunity cost. It was published on November 11, 2025. The repository holds show notes, not the conversation: neither the guest's exact wording nor the full context can be checked here.

The [mirrored discussion](../sources/articles/zhenyan-2012-03-19.md) contains exchanges dated March–April 2012. It preserves both readers' questions and replies attributed to Duan. Its filename is inherited from an earlier page label; it does not establish a March 19 publication date.

## Three roles to keep distinct

- **Operator:** examines product, customers, incentives, and the people who sustain a business.
- **Allocator:** compares uses of capital, time, and attention under uncertainty.
- **Public respondent:** answers a particular person's question in a particular context; a short reply is not necessarily a universal rule.

These roles are useful editorial lenses. They do not imply privileged access to his private thinking or a single finished doctrine.

## Reading the evidence

A mirrored reply can be closer to a first-person statement than a media paraphrase, but its authenticity and missing context still matter. A publisher's chapter summary is weaker evidence of exact speech. A compilation that cites older posts remains secondary until those posts are checked. An archive snippet is a discovery lead.

The [archive index](../sources/articles/zhenyan-archive.md) includes reposts attributed to other writers. A Duan account heading does not make those writers' words his own. Repeating a source under different filenames does not create corroboration.

Prefer a traceable statement with the surrounding question over a popular screenshot. Preserve changes over time and differences between operating a company and owning shares. Do not infer personal motives from a career outcome, and do not use admiration, scarcity of appearances, or a Buffett comparison as evidence.

## What remains unverified

This library does not independently establish a detailed education timeline, complete interview history, exact present holdings, private mentorship relationships, or every company's founding lineage. Earlier broad chronological claims were removed because the local captures do not substantiate them. Retrieve an appropriate primary record when one of these facts matters to the user's question.
