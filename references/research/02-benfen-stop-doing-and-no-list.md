# Benfen, Stop Doing, and the No-List

## Evidence and meaning

The [mirrored discussion](../sources/articles/zhenyan-2012-03-19.md) contains a reader's concern about culture being used to demand obedience and a reply attributed to Duan about love, respect, and a broader meaning of benfen. Keep the reader's formulation separate from the reply. Benfen is left transliterated because one English word such as honesty, duty, or obedience does not capture all of its uses.

The [Sina article](../sources/articles/sina-no-list.md) presents a stop-doing list for investing and business. It is secondary reporting: individual prohibitions reflect reported circumstances, not universal instructions for every user. The [PDF excerpt](../sources/articles/investment-logic-pdf-source-card.md) also gathers attributed discussions about choosing the right activity before improving execution, but its compiler and original-post trail are incomplete.

## Modern application

A no-list makes exclusions explicit before an attractive opportunity consumes resources. Separate three kinds of boundaries:

- The user's obligations and non-negotiable values, such as honest claims or timely payment.
- Risk limits that protect survival, such as a cash reserve or loss capacity.
- Revisable hypotheses, such as a distribution channel that currently fails to produce repeat demand.

The first two need concrete grounds; the third needs a condition under which it could be reconsidered. Do not convert the analyst's preference for caution into a user requirement.

For each proposed exclusion, explain the harm being avoided, the evidence, the foregone upside, and what could change the decision. A learning pilot can be acceptable even where a large commitment is not. A ban on learning would shrink competence instead of protecting it.

## Preserve the economic and cultural link

Promises matter through actual customer and employee consequences. Examine sales claims, refund behavior, supplier payment, incentives, and responses to bad news. Calling a company principled does not establish sound economics, and profitable behavior does not by itself make a broken promise acceptable.

Stop-doing discipline should reduce avoidable harm and free attention for useful work. It is not a requirement to do less for its own sake, a refusal of competition, or a defense of leaving known damage unaddressed.

## A useful question

A team proposes selling a feature it cannot yet deliver. Separate a clearly labeled prototype or preorder from a false claim that the feature exists. Preserve the user's revenue objective while comparing honest ways to test demand, the cost of development, and the cost of lost trust. This is a hypothetical modern application, not a historical Duan statement.
