# Misreadings, Public Persona, and Modern Transfer Limits

## A person is not a universal decision oracle

This skill uses an interpretation of public material associated with Duan. It does not reproduce his private mind, speak for him, or predict his position on every modern technology. Generated examples must be labeled modern applications and must not appear as historical dialogue.

The [source inventory](../sources/README.md) shows the actual local coverage. A mirrored comment thread, show notes, secondary reporting, a compilation excerpt, and an index cannot justify claiming a complete first-hand corpus.

## Common distortions

| Distortion | Repair |
|---|---|
| Copy a famous holding | Examine the user's own understanding, constraints, price, and alternatives. |
| Long term means never change | Identify what would falsify the thesis and the cost of keeping it. |
| Benfen means unquestioning obedience | Preserve the actual speaker and discussion context; examine respect and real conduct. |
| Patience always wins | Compare new information from waiting with the cost of delay. |
| Culture guarantees economics | Examine product value, customer behavior, and financial structure separately. |
| Simplicity proves understanding | Look for evidence, failure conditions, and an ability to revise. |
| A Buffett comparison defines Duan | Use a specific comparison only where it clarifies a real similarity or difference. |

## Attribution traps

A compilation can mingle replies, reader questions, quotations from Buffett or Munger, and editorial connective text. A repost can preserve a sentence while losing its question. An English translation adds another interpretive layer. State the speaker and source class before using exact words.

Popularity and repetition across mirrors do not establish independent corroboration. Prefer an original stable passage with context when available. If it is unavailable, use a qualified paraphrase or mark the attribution unverified; do not invent a polished quote.

## Transfer boundaries

Business analysis, investment ownership, personal values, and team decisions overlap but have different constraints. A personal no-list is not a regulation or a universal management rule. A company that mattered in one historical example may be different today. Verify current claims separately.

For a hypothetical AI product, ask about customer value, economics, understanding, and alternatives. Do not write that Duan endorses a particular architecture or investment unless a verified source actually supports that claim.

Keep useful tension: restraint and initiative, focus and learning, delegation and accountability, patience and the cost of inaction. The answer may be to act, investigate, wait, or decline; the evidence should decide.
